MC Trailhead

The starting point for playing on our servers.

What it is

MC Trailhead is a free, non-commercial desktop launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition, used by a small private community of invited players on our self-hosted servers. A player picks one of the servers we offer, and Trailhead installs the right Minecraft version, mod loader, Java runtime and modpack into its own folder, keeps the modpack in sync with the server, and launches the game straight into that server.

It is the companion to MC Server Manager, the tool we use to run the servers. Trailhead only connects to servers its users have been invited to. It is not sold, and has no ads or purchases.

Signing in

  • Players sign in with their own Microsoft account in their web browser (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE). Trailhead never sees their password.
  • Trailhead uses the sign-in to get a Minecraft access token, checks that the account owns Java Edition, reads the player's profile (name and UUID), and starts the official game.
  • A genuine account that owns Java Edition is required. There is no offline or cracked mode.
  • The game itself is downloaded only from Mojang's official servers and is not modified.

Privacy

  • Sign-in tokens stay on the player's computer, encrypted with the operating system's credential protection (DPAPI on Windows).
  • Tokens are only sent to Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft services. They are never sent to our servers or anyone else.
  • Trailhead does not collect analytics, track usage, or sell or share any data.
  • Our servers only see what any Minecraft server sees when a player joins (their name and UUID), plus which modpack files they download.
  • Signing out in Trailhead deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be removed at any time from your Microsoft account.
